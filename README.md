# 🎬 Netflix Data Analysis Project

## 📘 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Netflix's catalog of Movies and TV Shows to uncover **content trends, audience preferences, and strategic insights**. With streaming competition increasing, understanding how Netflix’s content has evolved across countries, genres, and formats is essential for data-driven decision-making.

Using Python and data visualization techniques, this project explores the Netflix dataset and provides insights that could guide future content strategy and production planning.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Netflix, as one of the leading OTT platforms, constantly expands its library with diverse content. However, with competitors like Amazon Prime and Disney+, it’s vital to analyze its catalog for:

* The balance between **Movies and TV Shows**
* The **popularity of genres** over time
* **Country-wise representation** of content

The objective is to identify strengths, gaps, and opportunities in Netflix’s content distribution to support strategic recommendations.

---

## 🧩 Objectives

* Analyze the distribution of **Movies vs. TV Shows** across years
* Identify the **most popular genres** and their evolution
* Examine **country-wise contributions** to Netflix’s catalog
* Provide **strategic insights** into content trends and audience targeting

---

## 📊 Dataset Description

**File:** `Netflix Dataset.csv`
**Size:** 7,789 records × 11 columns

**Key Columns:**

* `show_id`: Unique identifier for each show
* `type`: Movie or TV Show
* `title`: Name of the show
* `director`, `cast`: Creators and main cast
* `country`: Country of origin
* `date_added`: When it was added to Netflix
* `release_year`: Original release year
* `rating`: Age rating
* `duration`: Movie length or number of seasons
* `listed_in`: Genres or categories

**Data Range:** 2008 – 2021
**Source:** Public Netflix dataset from Kaggle
